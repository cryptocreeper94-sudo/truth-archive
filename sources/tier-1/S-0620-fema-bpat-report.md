# S-0620 — FEMA BPAT Report

## What it is
The FEMA BPAT Report is a comprehensive investigation into the collapses of the World Trade Center buildings, outlining findings on structural integrity and collapse mechanisms.

## Where it is held
National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), catalog number NIST NCSTAR 1-6.

## Verification
Available online at the NIST website, this report can be accessed through their publications section.
