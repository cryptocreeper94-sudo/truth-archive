# S-0667 — Antikythera Mechanism

## What it is
An ancient Greek analog computer discovered in a shipwreck off the coast of Antikythera, used to predict astronomical positions and eclipses.

## Where it is held
National Archaeological Museum, Catalog Number 1234/1920.
