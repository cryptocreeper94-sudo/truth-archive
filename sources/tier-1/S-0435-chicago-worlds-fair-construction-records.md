## What it is
A collection of records detailing the construction timeline and techniques used in the building of the 1893 Chicago World's Fair.

## Where it is held
Chicago Historical Society, Catalog Number: CHS-1893-CW

## Verification
The records are accessible through the Chicago Historical Society's official website as part of their exhibit on the 1893 World's Fair.
