# S-0594 — FIRMS Satellite Imagery Database

## What it is
A comprehensive database that provides real-time satellite imagery of active fires, including historical data before and after significant fire events.

## Where it is held
NASA MODIS/FIRMS, available online.

## Verification
The database can be accessed and verified at the NASA FIRMS website, where users can search for specific events by date and location.
