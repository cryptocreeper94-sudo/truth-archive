# S-0448 — MODIS Satellite Imagery of Lahaina

## What it is
Satellite imagery from the MODIS system showing pre- and post-fire conditions in Lahaina, useful for analyzing fire spread and impact.

## Where it is held
NASA, Catalog Number MODIS-2023-LAHAINA.

## Verification
The imagery can be accessed and verified through NASA’s MODIS data portal.
