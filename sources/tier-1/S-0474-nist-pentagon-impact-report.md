# S-0474 — NIST Report on Pentagon Impact

## What it is
A National Institute of Standards and Technology report detailing the investigation into the impact of Flight 77 on the Pentagon, including analysis of the entry hole dimensions.

## Where it is held
National Institute of Standards and Technology, NIST Report No. NCSTAR 2.

## Verification
This report can be accessed online through the NIST publications website.
