# S-0715 — WTC Dust Sample Analysis Reports

## What it is
This collection includes analysis reports of dust samples collected from the World Trade Center site, detailing the chemical composition and presence of thermitic materials.

## Where it is held
University of Alaska Fairbanks, available at https://www.uaf.edu/wtc7/dust-analysis/.
