# S-0388 — Foundling Hospital Archives

## What it is
A collection of records from the Foundling Hospital, including admission records, medical information, and correspondence regarding the children admitted.

## Where it is held
London Metropolitan Archives, Catalog number: LMA/4455.

## Verification
The archives can be accessed online or in person at the London Metropolitan Archives.
