# S-0392 — Hulsey WTC 7 Finite Element Study

## What it is
A finite element analysis study examining the structural integrity and failure mechanisms of WTC 7, concluding that fire alone could not account for the observed collapse.

## Where it is held
University of Alaska Fairbanks, Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering.

The study can be verified through the University of Alaska Fairbanks' official website and documentation regarding the project.
