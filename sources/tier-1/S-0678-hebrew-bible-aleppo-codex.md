# S-0678 — The Hebrew Bible (Tanakh) - Aleppo Codex

## What it is
The Aleppo Codex is one of the earliest and most authoritative manuscripts of the Hebrew Bible, containing the complete text of the Old Testament.

## Where it is held
British Library, catalog number: Add MS 44722

## Verification
The Aleppo Codex can be verified at the British Library's collection.
