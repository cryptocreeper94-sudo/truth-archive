# S-0713 — Eyewitness Reports on Pentagon Impact

## What it is
This collection includes eyewitness accounts and reports regarding the impact of the aircraft on the Pentagon on September 11, 2001.

## Where it is held
National Archives, document number 1999-3020, available online at https://www.archives.gov/research/guide-fed-records/records-2001-09-11-pentagon.
