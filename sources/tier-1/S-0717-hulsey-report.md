# S-0717 — Hulsey Report on WTC 7

## What it is
A detailed analysis of the collapse of World Trade Center 7, using finite element modeling to simulate the building's behavior under fire conditions.

## Where it is held
University of Alaska Fairbanks, catalog number UAF-WTC7-2020.
