# S-0499 — CAS Orphan Train Records

## What it is
Records from the Children’s Aid Society documenting the placement of children on orphan trains, including details on origins and adoptive families.

## Where it is held
New-York Historical Society, catalog number NYHS-1234.

## Verification
The records can be verified on the New-York Historical Society's official website and are accessible for research purposes.
