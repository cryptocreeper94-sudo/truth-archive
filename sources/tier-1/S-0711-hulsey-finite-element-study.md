# S-0711 — Hulsey Finite Element Study on WTC 7

## What it is
This study employs finite element methods to analyze the collapse of WTC 7, providing insights into the structural integrity of the building during the collapse.

## Where it is held
University of Alaska Fairbanks, Department of Civil Engineering, project page available at https://www.uaf.edu/wtc7/study/.
