# S-0348 — British Museum Tools Collection

## What it is
A collection of tools claimed to be from the Lower Paleolithic, found in sediment layers dated much older than expected, held at the British Museum.

## Where it is held
British Museum, catalog number 14567.

## Verification
The collection can be accessed through the British Museum's online catalog.
