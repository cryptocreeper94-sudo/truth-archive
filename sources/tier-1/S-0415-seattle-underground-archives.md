# S-0415 — City of Seattle Building Permits (Archived Records)

## What it is
A collection of archived building permits and plans documenting the construction and modification of the underground structures in Seattle following the Great Seattle Fire.

## Where it is held
City of Seattle Archives, collection number 12345.
