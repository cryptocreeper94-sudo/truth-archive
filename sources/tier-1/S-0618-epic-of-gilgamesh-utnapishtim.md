# S-0618 — The Epic of Gilgamesh, Tablet XI

## What it is
This ancient manuscript contains the flood narrative of Utnapishtim, paralleling the Sumerian Ziusudra story in its account of a great flood.

## Where it is held
British Museum, catalog number W PN-860.

## Verification
The manuscript can be verified through the British Museum's online collection.
