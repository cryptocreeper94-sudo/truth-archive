# S-0386 — CAS Archives on Orphan Trains

## What it is
A collection of archival material related to the Children's Aid Society's Orphan Train project, including letters, records, and reports regarding the care and placement of children.

## Where it is held
New York Public Library, Manuscripts and Archives Division. Catalog number: MssCol 1321.

## Verification
The archives can be accessed through the New York Public Library's website or in person at the library's Manuscripts and Archives Division.
