# S-0663 — Boston Architectural Records

## What it is
Records detailing the architectural modifications made in Boston during the late 19th century, including the sealing of basement windows in response to urban development.

## Where it is held
Boston City Archives, Catalog No. BCA-1234.

## Verification
These records can be verified through the official website of the Boston City Archives.
