# S-0710 — FEMA BPAT Report Appendix C on WTC

## What it is
This document contains the findings of the Building Performance Assessment Team (BPAT) regarding the structural performance of the World Trade Center buildings during the September 11 attacks.

## Where it is held
National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), available online at https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.NCSTAR.1-6.

## Verification
The document can be verified through the NIST website and is part of the official investigation into the WTC collapses.
