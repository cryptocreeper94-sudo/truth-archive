# S-0524 — Chicago World's Fair Commission Reports

## What it is
Official reports detailing the planning, construction timelines, and labor resources utilized during the 1893 Chicago World's Fair, providing context for the construction photographs and timelines.

## Where it is held
Chicago World's Fair Commission.

## Verification
These reports can be verified through the Chicago World's Fair Commission's archives.
