# S-0712 — Pentagon Building Performance Report

## What it is
This report assesses the structural performance of the Pentagon following the September 11 attacks, detailing the damage caused by the aircraft impact.

## Where it is held
National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), available online at https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.NCSTAR.1-5.

## Verification
The document can be verified through the NIST website and is part of the official investigation into the Pentagon attack.
