# S-0655 — 1880 Census Records

## What it is
These are the official census records from the United States for the year 1880, which document the population, including children, across various states.

## Where it is held
National Archives, catalog number: 1880CEN

## Verification
The records can be verified through the National Archives website, which provides access to the census data and its historical context.
