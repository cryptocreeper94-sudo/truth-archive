# S-0673 — The Gospel of Thomas: The Hidden Sayings of Jesus

## What it is
A manuscript from the Nag Hammadi library containing sayings attributed to Jesus, significant for understanding early Christian thought.

## Where it is held
The British Museum, Catalog Number 85708.
