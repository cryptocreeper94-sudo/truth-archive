# C-0184 — Cloud Seeding Programs by State/Country

## The claim
Various states and countries have implemented cloud seeding programs documented through government contracts. For instance, Wyoming's cloud seeding initiative is backed by state legislation and specific funding allocations.

## The evidence
- **S-0533** — Wyoming Department of Environmental Quality cloud seeding program documentation, catalog number: WDEQ-2022-CloudSeeding, verify-at: https://deq.wyoming.gov/air-quality/cloud-seeding-program/
- **S-0534** — UAE Weather Modification Program report, catalog number: UAE-WMP-2021, verify-at: https://www.uaeweather.ae/en/weather-modification

## Objection at full strength
Opponents of cloud seeding argue that it lacks transparency and may lead to legal disputes over water rights. Furthermore, the scientific community is divided on its efficacy, with some studies suggesting minimal impact on precipitation levels compared to the resources expended.
