# C-0169 — Pentagon entry hole dimensions vs aircraft fuselage

## The claim
The dimensions of the entry hole at the Pentagon resulting from the impact of Flight 77 do not match the expected dimensions of a Boeing 757 fuselage, suggesting that the official narrative of the aircraft's impact may not be accurate.

## The evidence
1. **Pentagon Building Performance Report**: This document provides detailed descriptions of the damage at the Pentagon, including the dimensions of the entry hole.
   - **Holder**: United States Department of Defense
   - **Catalog Reference**: DoD Report No. 2001-1
   - **Verify at**: https://www.defense.gov/Portals/1/Documents/pubs/Pentagon%20Building%20Performance%20Report.pdf

2. **NIST Report on Pentagon Impact**: This report details findings from the NIST investigation regarding the impact of Flight 77, including assessments of the entry hole.
   - **Holder**: National Institute of Standards and Technology
   - **Catalog Reference**: NIST Report No. NCSTAR 2
   - **Verify at**: https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/NCSTAR/ncstar2.pdf

## Objection at full strength
Official reports maintain that the entry hole dimensions were consistent with the impact of Flight 77, arguing that the fuselage could have penetrated the building at an angle, thus affecting the hole's size and shape.
