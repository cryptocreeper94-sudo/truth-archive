# C-0145 — Thermite Residue Analysis in WTC

## The claim
Analysis of dust samples from the World Trade Center site has shown the presence of thermite residues, suggesting the possible use of incendiary materials in the collapse of WTC 1 and 2, which is not accounted for in the official reports.

## The evidence
1. Harrit, N., et al. (2009). "Active Thermitic Material Discovered in Dust from the 9/11 World Trade Center Catastrophe". Bentham Open.
2. NIST FOIA documents related to the analysis of post-collapse debris, which may contain further insights into the chemical composition of the dust.

## Objection at full strength
Skeptics argue that the presence of thermite residues could be a result of common construction materials and that its detection does not imply controlled demolition, pointing to the absence of conclusive evidence linking thermite to the collapse mechanisms as outlined in NIST investigations.
