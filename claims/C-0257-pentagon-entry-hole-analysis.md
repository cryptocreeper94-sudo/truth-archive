# C-0257 — Pentagon Entry Hole Analysis vs Aircraft Fuselage

## The claim
Analysis of the Pentagon entry hole dimensions raises questions concerning the size of the aircraft fuselage involved in the incident. The observed entry hole is significantly smaller than what would be expected from a Boeing 757, suggesting either an alternative explanation for the damage or evidence of an anomaly in the physical event. 

## The evidence
- Pentagon Building Performance Report, National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), document number NIST NCSTAR 1-5, 2005.
- Eyewitness accounts and structural analysis reports archived at the National Archives, document number 1999-3020.

## Objection at full strength
Opponents assert that the entry hole size is consistent with high-speed impacts of large aircraft and that the discrepancies can be attributed to the structural damage and debris patterns resulting from the impact. They argue that the analysis does not take into account the complexity of the impact dynamics.
