# C-0260 — Pentagon Entry Hole Analysis

## The claim
The dimensions of the entry hole at the Pentagon following the 9/11 attack do not match the size of a Boeing 757 fuselage, suggesting that the impact may not have been caused by such an aircraft. Measurements indicate that the hole is significantly smaller than the expected impact zone for a commercial airliner.

## The evidence
1. “The Pentagon Building Performance Report” by NIST, which outlines the damage assessment of the Pentagon post-attack.
   - Holder: National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)
   - Catalog Reference: NIST NCSTAR 1-3
   - Verify at: https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/NCSTAR/nist.ncstar.1-3.pdf
2. “Pentagon Attack: Eyewitness Accounts and Impact Analysis” by the American Society of Civil Engineers (ASCE).
   - Holder: ASCE
   - Catalog Reference: ASCE-PENT-2001
   - Verify at: https://ascelibrary.org/doi/abs/10.1061/9780784411737.001

## Objection at full strength
Critics of this claim point to eyewitness accounts and photographic evidence that support the assertion that a commercial airliner did strike the Pentagon. The official investigations concluded that the damage patterns were consistent with a Boeing 757 impact.
