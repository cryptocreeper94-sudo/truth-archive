# C-0245 — WTC 1/2/7 Freefall Analysis

## The claim
The WTC 1, 2, and 7 buildings exhibited periods of freefall acceleration, which cannot be attributed solely to office fires. The FEMA BPAT Report Appendix C identifies sulfidation and intergranular melting of steel as phenomena not explained by typical office fires, suggesting an alternative cause for the collapses.

## The evidence
1. FEMA BPAT Report Appendix C: Document stating the findings related to the structural integrity of steel during the WTC collapse. 
   - Holder: FEMA
   - Catalog Number: FEMA-403
   - Verification URL: https://www.fema.gov/media-library-data/20130726-1920-25045-1857/fema403.pdf

2. Hulsey, J. (2020). "Final Report on the Collapse of World Trade Center Building 7." University of Alaska Fairbanks. Peer-reviewed study evaluating the collapse mechanisms of WTC 7.
   - Holder: University of Alaska Fairbanks
   - Catalog Number: UAF-WTC7-2020
   - Verification URL: https://www.uaf.edu/wtc7/

## Objection at full strength
Critics argue that freefall periods can be explained by the rapid descent of the structures without requiring external forces, suggesting that the observed phenomena could still align with the official narrative of a structural failure caused by fire and impact damage rather than demolition or other external factors.
