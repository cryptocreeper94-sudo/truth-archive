# C-0261 — Thermite Residue Analysis

## The claim
Analysis conducted by Harrit et al. (2009) found evidence of thermite residue in the dust from the World Trade Center debris, suggesting that explosives were used to facilitate the collapse of the towers rather than solely the result of fire-induced structural failure.

## The evidence
1. Harrit, N., et al. (2009). “Active Thermitic Material Discovered in Dust from the 9/11 World Trade Center Catastrophe.” Bentham Open.
   - Holder: Bentham Science Publishers
   - Catalog Reference: BENTHAM-2009
   - Verify at: https://www.benthamscience.com/journal/contents/volume/1/issue/1/ 
2. NIST NCSTAR 1A, which does not address the presence of thermitic material.
   - Holder: National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)
   - Catalog Reference: NIST NCSTAR 1A
   - Verify at: https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/NCSTAR/nist.ncstar.1A.pdf

## Objection at full strength
Opponents of the thermite hypothesis argue that the residues found could be attributed to the normal combustion processes or the building's materials rather than indicating the use of explosives. NIST has stated that their investigations did not find any evidence supporting the use of explosives in the collapse of the buildings.
