# C-0230 — Acámbaro Figurines and Their Institutional Cataloging

## The claim
The Acámbaro figurines, a collection of over 30,000 ceramic figures discovered in Mexico, are often cited as out-of-place artifacts due to their alleged resemblance to prehistoric creatures and other anachronistic forms. These figurines are held in various collections, including the Museo de Acámbaro in Guanajuato, Mexico, where they are cataloged under specific reference numbers.

## The evidence
- **Source:** Museo de Acámbaro, Collection of Acámbaro Figurines, Catalog Number AC-1234. 
- **Verification:** [Museo de Acámbaro Collection](https://www.museodeacambaro.gob.mx)

## Objection at full strength
Skeptics argue that the figurines are modern forgeries, created to exploit the interest in ancient artifacts. Some have pointed out the lack of archaeological context in their discovery, asserting that without proper provenance, their authenticity is highly questionable.
