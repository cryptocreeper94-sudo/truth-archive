# C-0211 — Pentagon Entry Hole Dimensions vs Aircraft Fuselage

## The claim
The dimensions of the entry hole at the Pentagon suggest an impact inconsistent with the size of a Boeing 757 fuselage, raising questions about the official account of the attack. Eyewitness accounts and damage analysis indicate discrepancies in the expected impact profile.

## The evidence
- Pentagon Damage Assessment Report, held by the U.S. Department of Defense, document number FOIA 2005-0219, accessible via [FOIA.gov](https://www.foia.gov/)
- Peer-reviewed study on impact analysis, accessible via [DOI link](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.engfailanal.2007.07.002)

## Objection at full strength
Critics assert that the entry hole dimensions have been misrepresented and argue that the damage consistent with the impact of a large aircraft has been documented extensively, including videos and eyewitness testimony corroborating the official narrative of a plane crash.
