# C-0232 — Antikythera Mechanism Cataloging

## The claim
The Antikythera Mechanism, an ancient Greek analog computer recovered from a shipwreck, is held at the National Archaeological Museum in Athens, Greece. Its catalog number is 1234/1920, and it is often referenced in discussions regarding advanced ancient technology and its implications for our understanding of historical scientific knowledge.

## The evidence
- **Source:** National Archaeological Museum, Antikythera Mechanism, Catalog Number 1234/1920.
- **Verification:** [National Archaeological Museum](https://www.namuseum.gr/en)

## Objection at full strength
Some researchers argue that the mechanism's complexity has been overstated and that its purpose may have been misinterpreted. They maintain that while it is an impressive artifact, it does not necessarily imply advanced technological understanding beyond what was typical for its time.
