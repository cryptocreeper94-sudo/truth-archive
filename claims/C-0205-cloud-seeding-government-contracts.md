# C-0205 — Cloud seeding programs by government contracts

## The claim
Various government contracts exist for cloud seeding programs designed to enhance precipitation and mitigate drought conditions. These contracts detail state and national initiatives in regions such as Wyoming, UAE, and China.

## The evidence
1. **Contract Document: Wyoming Cloud Seeding Program** - Wyoming Department of Agriculture. [Wyoming Cloud Seeding Contract](https://www.wyomingcloudseeding.com)
2. **Report: Cloud Seeding in the UAE** - UAE National Center of Meteorology. [UAE Cloud Seeding Report](https://www.ncm.ae/en/cloud-seeding)

## Objection at full strength
Despite the existence of cloud seeding contracts, the efficacy and safety of these programs remain subjects of debate. Detractors argue that cloud seeding can have limited effectiveness and raises ethical issues regarding the manipulation of weather patterns, as well as concerns about environmental impacts and unintended consequences.
