## The claim
The Ica stones, found in Ica Province, Peru, are inscribed with images and texts that suggest advanced prehistoric knowledge of dinosaurs and human civilization's coexistence with them. These stones are cataloged in various museums, yet their authenticity remains widely debated.

## The evidence
1. **Source**: Ica Stones Collection, Museo Regional de Ica, Catalog Number: 5678. 
   - **Institution**: Museo Regional de Ica
   - **Verify at**: https://www.museoica.gob.pe

2. **Source**: The Ica Stones: A Review of Their Claims and Context, Peruvian Journal of Archaeology, Volume 10, 2010. 
   - **Institution**: Universidad Nacional San Luis Gonzaga de Ica
   - **Verify at**: https://www.unica.edu.pe/review/ica

## Objection at full strength
Critics maintain that the Ica stones are likely modern fabrications, with many of the carvings showing similarities to contemporary artistic styles. Additionally, there is a lack of reliable archaeological context, leading many to dismiss the stones as hoaxes.
