# C-0164 — Pentagon Entry Hole Dimensions vs Aircraft Fuselage

## The claim
The dimensions of the entry hole in the Pentagon following the 9/11 attacks appear inconsistent with the size of a Boeing 757 fuselage. Analysis suggests that the hole is significantly smaller than the expected impact size, raising questions about the nature of the event that caused the damage.

## The evidence
1. Pentagon Damage Assessment Report by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers (2001), cataloged under NIST FOIA document number NIST FOIA 2002-002.
2. Engineering analysis published in the Journal of 9/11 Studies, available at [Journal of 9/11 Studies](https://www.journalof911studies.com).

## Objection at full strength
Critics maintain that eyewitness accounts and videos of the event corroborate the official story of a plane crash, and that the damage pattern can be reconciled with the size of a Boeing 757, thus disputing claims of inconsistency.
