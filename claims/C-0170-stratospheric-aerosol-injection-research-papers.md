# C-0170 — Stratospheric aerosol injection research papers

## The claim
Research papers from the Harvard Solar Geoengineering Research Program document the feasibility and potential impacts of stratospheric aerosol injection (SAI) as a geoengineering technique to combat climate change. Specific studies include assessments of environmental impacts and effectiveness in temperature reduction.

## The evidence
- **Source**: Harvard Solar Geoengineering Research Program. 
  - **Title**: Stratospheric Aerosol Injection: A Review of the Literature 
  - **Institution**: Harvard University
  - **Date of Object**: 2021
  - **Verify at**: https://geoengineering.environment.harvard.edu/publications

## Objection at full strength
Critics argue that stratospheric aerosol injection poses significant risks to global weather patterns and could lead to unintended consequences, including disruption of monsoons and other critical climate systems. Additionally, the ethical implications of deliberately manipulating the climate are heavily contested.
