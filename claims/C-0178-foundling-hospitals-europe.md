# C-0178 — Historical Documentation of Foundling Hospitals in Europe

## The claim
Foundling hospitals across Europe, particularly in the 18th and 19th centuries, served as critical institutions for the care of abandoned children, with records indicating varying degrees of success in child welfare, depending on the institution's management and funding.

## The evidence
1. **Foundling Hospital Records** - The Foundling Hospital in London, established in 1739, holds extensive records of admissions, care, and outcomes of children. These records can be accessed via the London Metropolitan Archives, catalog number LMA/4453. [Verification URL](https://www.cityoflondon.gov.uk)
2. **Historical Analysis** - Scholarly articles analyzing foundling hospitals provide context for understanding their role and effectiveness in child welfare, with references available from the British Library. 

## Objection at full strength
Some historians dispute the reliability of foundling hospital records, noting that many documents have been lost or destroyed over time, which may lead to skewed interpretations of their effectiveness and the true scale of abandonment.
