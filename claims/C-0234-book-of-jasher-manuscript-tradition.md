## The claim
The Book of Jasher is referenced in biblical texts and is thought to have been composed during the early Second Temple period, with various manuscript copies existing, notably one in the British Library.

## The evidence

## Objection at full strength
Skeptics argue that the Book of Jasher lacks credible manuscript evidence prior to the 18th century, suggesting it may be a modern fabrication or heavily edited reinterpretation of earlier texts, which undermines claims regarding its authenticity and ancient origins.
