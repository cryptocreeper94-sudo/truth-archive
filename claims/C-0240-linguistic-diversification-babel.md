# C-0240 — Linguistic Diversification and the Tower of Babel Narrative

## The claim
The Tower of Babel narrative in Genesis presents a theological explanation for linguistic diversification, suggesting that the multiplicity of languages arose from divine intervention. This contrasts with contemporary linguistic studies that indicate gradual language development through historical and social processes.

## The evidence
- **Source**: The Book of Genesis (Tanakh)
  - **Holder**: The Jewish Publication Society
  - **Date of Object**: ~5th century BCE
  - **Verification**: Available at [The Jewish Virtual Library](https://www.jewishvirtuallibrary.org/the-book-of-genesis)

## Objection at full strength
Critics of the Babel narrative argue that linguistic diversification is better explained by historical and social factors rather than a singular divine event. The complexity of language evolution suggests multiple influences, making the biblical account an oversimplified interpretation of a multifaceted phenomenon.
