# C-0173 — Underreporting of Canadian Wildfires (2023-2026)

## The claim
There is a significant underreporting of Canadian wildfires, particularly from 2023 to 2026, in US media, which may obscure the scale and impact of these events. Analysis of media coverage suggests a disparity in attention given to these incidents compared to similar events in the US.

## The evidence
1. **Source S-0482**: Analysis of media reports and coverage frequency for Canadian wildfires compared to US wildfires during the same period, indicating a lack of proportional reporting.
   - **Holder**: Media Research Center
   - **Date of object**: 2023
   - **Verification**: [Media Coverage Analysis](https://www.mediaresearchcenter.org/)

2. **Source S-0483**: Government records from Canadian wildfires showing extensive fire activity and response efforts not adequately covered in US news outlets.
   - **Holder**: Government of Canada, Natural Resources Canada
   - **Date of object**: 2023
   - **Verification**: [Natural Resources Canada](https://www.nrcan.gc.ca/)

## Objection at full strength
The perception of underreporting may stem from differences in news priorities, audience interest, and the magnitude of events compared to US wildfires. Furthermore, media coverage may be influenced by accessibility and the availability of information rather than intentional omission.
