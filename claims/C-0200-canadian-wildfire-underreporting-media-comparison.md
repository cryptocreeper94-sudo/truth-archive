# C-0200 — Canadian Wildfire Underreporting in US Media Coverage (2023-2026)

## The claim
This claim posits that major US media outlets have significantly underreported coverage of Canadian wildfires during the years 2023-2026, especially in comparison to other disasters of similar scale. The volume of coverage appears disproportionately low relative to the event scale, which is documented through comparative analysis of coverage metrics across different platforms.

## Objection at full strength
Counter-arguments may assert that the perceived underreporting is a result of editorial choices based on audience interest, resource allocation, and the nature of the events themselves. Furthermore, media outlets may claim that their coverage is proportional to the immediate impact and visibility of the events, suggesting that not all disasters warrant equal media attention.
