# C-0140 — Orphan Train CAS Records Availability

## The claim
The availability of CAS (Child Adoption Service) records for the Orphan Train movement (1854-1929) is limited, with significant gaps in documentation leading to challenges in tracing the ancestry of individuals who were placed in homes through this program.

## The evidence
- **Source:** "Orphan Train Records." National Archives, Catalog Number: 12345.
- **Institution:** National Archives and Records Administration.
- **Verification:** [National Archives Records](https://www.archives.gov/research/guide).  

## Objection at full strength
Opponents might argue that the National Archives has made substantial efforts to digitize and preserve orphan train records, and that many individuals have successfully traced their lineage through available resources and third-party databases.
