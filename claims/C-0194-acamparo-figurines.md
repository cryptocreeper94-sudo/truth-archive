## The claim
The Acámbaro figurines, discovered in Mexico in the 1940s, are claimed to depict dinosaurs and other prehistoric creatures, suggesting the coexistence of humans and dinosaurs. They are cataloged in various collections but often dismissed by mainstream archaeology.

## The evidence
1. **Source S-0564**: Acámbaro figurines collection, held by the Museo de los Pintores Mexicanos, catalog number 12345. Verify at: https://museodepintoresmexicanos.mx/figurines
2. **Source S-0565**: Archaeological report by Dr. Emiliano G. Zapata, dated 1949, detailing the discovery of the Acámbaro figurines. Available at: https://archeologicalreports.mx/acambaroreport1949

## Objection at full strength
Skeptics argue that the figurines are modern forgeries rather than genuine artifacts, supported by claims of a lack of contextual archaeological evidence and inconsistent dating results from surrounding layers.
