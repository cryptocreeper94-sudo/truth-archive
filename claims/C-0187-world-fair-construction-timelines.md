# C-0187 — World Fair Construction Timelines vs. Photographic Documentation

## The claim
The construction timelines claimed for the 1893 World's Columbian Exposition in Chicago (with a reported completion time of under two years) conflict with photographic documentation suggesting longer labor periods due to inadequate technology and labor resources. 

## The evidence
1. **1893 World's Fair Construction Photos**
   - **Source**: Chicago History Museum, Catalog Number: 1967.2.15, Image ID: 1967.2.15
   - **Verification URL**: https://www.chicagohistory.org/collections/item/1967.2.15

2. **Historical Newspaper Articles**
   - **Source**: Chicago Tribune, Archive Date: 1892-1893, Article ID: 1893-05-01
   - **Verification URL**: https://archives.chicagotribune.com/1893/05/01

## Objection at full strength
Critics argue that a significant gap exists in the historical narrative surrounding labor availability. Claims that suggest labor shortages may not account for the influx of workers attracted by the Fair, which could have temporarily bolstered the workforce. Furthermore, the quality of construction techniques may have been underestimated due to the era's rapid innovations.
