# C-0166 — Discrepancies in Construction Timeline Photos for 19th-Century Structures

## The claim
Some major 19th-century structures, such as the Brooklyn Bridge and the Eiffel Tower, have construction timeline photos that contradict the claimed technology and labor availability of the time. For example, some images show large-scale construction efforts that seem inconsistent with the stated labor force and tools available in the 1880s.

## The evidence
- **Source 1:** 1890 construction photo of the Brooklyn Bridge, Library of Congress, Catalog number: LC-BH83- 0001. Verify at: https://www.loc.gov/item/2012649260/
- **Source 2:** Photo documentation of the Eiffel Tower's construction, 1887-1889, Library of Congress, Catalog number: LC-DIG-ppmsca-03252. Verify at: https://www.loc.gov/item/2001700958/

## Objection at full strength
Critics argue that the photos are simply artifacts of the time and do not necessarily reflect the actual construction process. The technology and labor force may have been capable of achieving what is depicted, and the context of the images may not fully represent the overall timeline.
