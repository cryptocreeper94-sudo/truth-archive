# C-0256 — WTC Freefall Analysis and Anomalies

## The claim
The analysis of the freefall of WTC 1, 2, and 7 indicates that the structural failures cannot be explained solely by office fires. The FEMA BPAT Report Appendix C highlights evidence of sulfidation and intergranular melting of steel that contradicts the official narratives. The University of Alaska Fairbanks finite element study (Hulsey 2020) supports these findings, revealing discrepancies in the structural integrity claims. 

## The evidence
- FEMA BPAT Report Appendix C, National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), document number NIST NCSTAR 1-6, 2005.
- Hulsey, J. (2020). Analysis of WTC 7 using finite element methods. University of Alaska Fairbanks. Available at: https://www.uaf.edu/wtc7/study/ 

## Objection at full strength
Critics argue that the observed phenomena can be attributed to fire-induced damage and that the conclusions drawn from the finite element analysis are speculative. They maintain that the NIST reports provide a comprehensive explanation for the collapses and that the evidence of sulfidation and melting, while noted, does not necessarily imply a controlled demolition or other anomalous cause of failure.
