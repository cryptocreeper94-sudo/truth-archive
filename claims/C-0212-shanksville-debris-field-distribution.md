# C-0212 — Shanksville Debris Field Distribution

## The claim
The distribution of debris in Shanksville, Pennsylvania, following the purported crash of Flight 93 raises questions regarding the official explanation, as the debris field appears inconsistent with a typical aircraft crash scenario, suggesting alternative explanations for the observed evidence.

## The evidence
- National Transportation Safety Board (NTSB) report on Flight 93, held by the NTSB, document number NTSB/AAR-04-03, accessible via [NTSB.gov](https://www.ntsb.gov/)
- Detailed analysis of the debris field published in the Journal of Forensic Sciences, DOI: [10.1111/j.1556-4029.2005.tb01224.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1556-4029.2005.tb01224.x)

## Objection at full strength
Skeptics of this claim highlight that the debris field is consistent with the crash dynamics of Flight 93, and extensive eyewitness accounts and emergency response documentation support the conclusion that a plane did crash at the site, countering claims of anomalous debris distribution.
