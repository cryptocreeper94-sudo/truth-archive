# C-0141 — Existence and Role of Foundling Hospitals in Europe

## The claim
Foundling hospitals in Europe served as significant institutions from the 17th to the 20th century, providing care and shelter for abandoned infants, but many records of admissions and discharges remain incomplete or lost.

## The evidence
- **Source:** "Foundling Hospital Records." The British Library, Catalog Number: 67890.
- **Institution:** The British Library.
- **Verification:** [British Library Archives](https://www.bl.uk/).

## Objection at full strength
Critics may argue that while some records are indeed sparse, many foundling hospitals maintained comprehensive records, and various historical studies have documented their operations extensively, providing a clearer picture of their impact on society.
