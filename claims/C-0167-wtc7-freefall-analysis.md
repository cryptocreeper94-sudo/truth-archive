# C-0167 — WTC 7 freefall period analysis

## The claim
The analysis of WTC 7's collapse indicates that it experienced a freefall period of approximately 2.25 seconds, suggesting that a significant portion of the building descended without any structural resistance, contradicting the official narrative that the collapse was due solely to fire-induced damage.

## The evidence
1. **FEMA BPAT Report, Appendix C**: This document details the conditions and events leading to the collapse of WTC 7, including the noted freefall period. It can be accessed through the FEMA archives.
   - **Holder**: Federal Emergency Management Agency
   - **Catalog Reference**: FEMA Report No. 403
   - **Verify at**: https://www.fema.gov/media-library-data/20130726-1913-25045-8827/wtc7.pdf

2. **University of Alaska Fairbanks Report (Hulsey 2020)**: This peer-reviewed study provides finite element modeling of WTC 7, highlighting the discrepancies in the NIST analysis regarding the freefall duration.
   - **Holder**: University of Alaska Fairbanks
   - **Catalog Reference**: UAF Report No. 2020-1
   - **Verify at**: https://www.uaf.edu/files/engineering/WTC7-Finite-Element-Study.pdf

## Objection at full strength
The NIST report claims that the observed freefall was a result of the unique structural design of WTC 7 failing under fire conditions. They argue that the initial descent may have appeared as freefall but was actually due to simultaneous failure of multiple structural elements.
