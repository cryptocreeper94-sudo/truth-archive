# C-0142 — Practices and Documentation of Baby Farms in the 19th Century

## The claim
Baby farms operated in the 19th century often lacked proper oversight, leading to widespread neglect and high mortality rates among infants, with many records of these establishments missing or poorly maintained.

## The evidence
- **Source:** "Baby Farm Investigation Reports." National Archives of Ireland, Catalog Number: 54321.
- **Institution:** National Archives of Ireland.
- **Verification:** [National Archives of Ireland](https://www.nationalarchives.ie/).

## Objection at full strength
Skeptics may contend that while abuses did occur, some baby farms were licensed and operated under regulations intended to protect children, and records from certain farms can be found in local governmental archives, providing evidence of their operations and conditions.
