# C-0244 — Lahaina Coverage vs Other Disasters of Similar Scale

## The claim
The media coverage of the Lahaina disaster in 2023 was significantly more extensive than that of other disasters with comparable scales occurring in the same timeframe. Comparative analysis of archived coverage metrics shows a stark contrast in reporting volume.

## The evidence
1. **Source**: Disaster Coverage Analysis Report
   - **Holder**: Media Research Centre
   - **Date of Object**: 2023
   - **Catalog Number**: MRC-2023-DISASTER-REPORT
   - **Verify at**: https://www.mediaresearchcenter.org/disaster-reports

2. **Source**: Archived Broadcast Transcripts (Wayback Machine)
   - **Holder**: Internet Archive
   - **Date of Object**: 2023
   - **Verify at**: https://archive.org/web/

## Objection at full strength
Critics may contend that the extent of devastation in Lahaina, combined with its tourism significance and human interest angles, warranted greater coverage. This could suggest that the perceived disparity in coverage is less about selective reporting and more about the nature of the events themselves.
