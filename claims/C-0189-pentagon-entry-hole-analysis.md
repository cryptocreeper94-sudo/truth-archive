# C-0189 — Pentagon Entry Hole Analysis vs Aircraft Fuselage

## The claim
The dimensions of the entry hole at the Pentagon post-9/11 do not correspond with the fuselage width of the Boeing 757, suggesting either a discrepancy in the impact analysis or the possibility of alternative explanations for the damage observed.

## The evidence
1. **Pentagon Damage Assessment Report** - This report details the physical damage at the Pentagon and includes measurements of the entry hole.
   - **Institution**: National Defense University
   - **Catalog Number**: N/A
   - **Verification URL**: https://www.ndu.edu/Portals/82/Documents/education/Pentagon_Report.pdf

2. **NIST NCSTAR 1-1 Report** - This comprehensive report on the Pentagon attack provides engineering analysis and impact assessments.
   - **Institution**: National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)
   - **Catalog Number**: NIST NCSTAR 1-1
   - **Verification URL**: https://www.nist.gov/publications-archive

## Objection at full strength
The official narrative maintains that the impact of American Airlines Flight 77, traveling at high speed, created sufficient force to produce the observed damage. The entry hole dimensions are justified by the angle of impact and the energy released during the collision, suggesting that the analysis of the damage aligns with the impact of a commercial airliner.
