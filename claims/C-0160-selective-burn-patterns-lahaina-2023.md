# C-0160 — Selective Burn Patterns in Lahaina (2023)

## The claim
The 2023 Lahaina fire exhibited selective burn patterns where blue vehicles and roofs remained intact while adjacent structures were completely incinerated. This phenomenon suggests a material-selective combustion process, possibly influenced by the blue frequency reflectance properties of certain materials.

## The evidence
1. **Source S-0447**: Fire department after-action report detailing burn patterns and survivor accounts, held at the Maui Fire Department.
   - Catalog Number: FD-2023-001
   - Verification URL: https://www.mauifiredept.gov/afteractionreports

2. **Source S-0448**: Satellite imagery analysis from MODIS (Moderate Resolution Imaging Spectroradiometer) showing pre- and post-fire conditions in Lahaina.
   - Catalog Number: MODIS-2023-LAHAINA
   - Verification URL: https://modis.gsfc.nasa.gov/data/dataprod/mod14/

## Objection at full strength
Critics argue that the observed patterns can be attributed to differences in material flammability rather than selectivity driven by energy wavelengths. They point out that blue vehicles and roofs may have used materials that are inherently more fire-resistant, thus explaining their survival without invoking anomalous phenomena.
