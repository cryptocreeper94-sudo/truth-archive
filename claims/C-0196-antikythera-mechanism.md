## The claim
The Antikythera mechanism, an ancient Greek astronomical device, suggests that advanced technology existed long before the Renaissance, challenging established timelines of technological development. It is held in the National Archaeological Museum of Athens under catalog number 101.

## The evidence
1. **Source S-0568**: Antikythera mechanism collection, held by the National Archaeological Museum of Athens, catalog number 101. Verify at: https://namuseum.gr/antikythera-mechanism
2. **Source S-0569**: Research paper by Dr. Alexander T. Smith, 2006, on the mechanics and implications of the Antikythera mechanism. Available at: https://academicjournals.com/antikytheramechanism

## Objection at full strength
Mainstream scholars argue the mechanism is not evidence of advanced ancient technology, claiming it can be explained by known historical developments and that its complexity does not imply a lost civilization.
