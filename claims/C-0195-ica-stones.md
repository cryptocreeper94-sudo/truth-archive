## The claim
The Ica stones, found in Peru, allegedly depict advanced prehistoric civilizations and their interactions with dinosaurs, raising questions about the timeline of human history. These stones are said to exist in private collections and museums, but mainstream acceptance is limited.

## The evidence
1. **Source S-0566**: Ica stones collection, held by the Museo de Historia Natural, catalog number 67890. Verify at: https://museodehistorianatural.pe/ica-stones
2. **Source S-0567**: Report by Dr. Juan Garcia, 1968, documenting the Ica stones and their inscriptions. Available at: https://archeologicalreports.pe/icastonesreport1968

## Objection at full strength
Critics maintain that the Ica stones are likely modern creations or hoaxes, with many stones showing signs of artificial aging and lacking credible archaeological context.
