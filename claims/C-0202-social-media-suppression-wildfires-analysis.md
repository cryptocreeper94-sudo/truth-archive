# C-0202 — Analysis of Social Media Suppression Patterns Regarding Wildfires

## The claim
This claim explores the patterns of suppression on social media platforms regarding Canadian wildfires, suggesting that posts and coverage may have been selectively limited or moderated compared to other disasters during the years 2023-2026.

## Objection at full strength
Critics may argue that social media platforms regularly moderate content based on their community guidelines and that any perceived suppression is a function of automated systems rather than a deliberate effort to limit discourse on specific topics. Additionally, the volume of content generated may overwhelm moderation capacities, leading to unintended gaps in visibility rather than intentional suppression.
