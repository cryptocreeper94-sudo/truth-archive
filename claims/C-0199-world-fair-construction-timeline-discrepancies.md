# C-0199 — World Fair Construction Photos vs Claimed Timelines

## The claim
Photographic evidence of the construction of various World Fairs suggests that the timelines claimed for completion of these structures were significantly underestimated. Notable discrepancies arise when comparing photographic documentation of the 1893 World's Columbian Exposition and the 1900 Exposition Universelle against their claimed construction timelines.

## The evidence
- **World's Columbian Exposition documentation**: The Library of Congress contains photographic records with image ID [LC-DIG-ppmsca-05710](https://www.loc.gov/pictures/item/2001690249/) that depict ongoing construction activities long after the claimed completion dates.
- **Exposition Universelle records**: Additional photographs in the same archive, such as [LC-DIG-ppmsca-05711](https://www.loc.gov/pictures/item/2001690274/), similarly illustrate ongoing works that challenge the official timelines.

## Objection at full strength
Opponents of this claim assert that construction may have continued post-official opening for aesthetic or functional reasons, and that the images could represent activities unrelated to the primary construction timeline. Furthermore, the timeline discrepancies might be due to different interpretations of what constitutes the completion of a structure, complicating the assessment of photographic evidence against claimed timelines.
