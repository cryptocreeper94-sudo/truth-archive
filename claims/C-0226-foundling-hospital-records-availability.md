# C-0226 — Foundling Hospital Records Availability

## The claim
Records from foundling hospitals in Europe indicate that many entries were lost or destroyed, particularly during periods of war and social upheaval. These missing records hinder research into the lives of children admitted to such institutions, creating gaps in the historical narrative surrounding orphaned and abandoned children.

## The evidence
- **Source S-0657**: Records of the Foundling Hospital in London, held at The National Archives, which detail the systematic destruction of certain records during World War II.
- **Source S-0658**: Reports from the Foundling Hospital's archives indicating a lack of documentation for many children admitted during the late 19th century.

## Objection at full strength
The destruction of records may be attributed to administrative decisions made during crises, rather than malicious intent. Additionally, some historical records may have been poorly maintained and lost to time rather than directly destroyed.
