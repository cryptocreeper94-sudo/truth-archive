# C-0168 — Thermite residue analysis in WTC dust

## The claim
Analysis of dust samples from the World Trade Center site revealed the presence of thermitic materials, suggesting that explosives may have been used in the destruction of the towers, which contradicts the official explanation attributing the collapse to fire and impact damage.

## The evidence
1. **Harrit et al. (2009) Study**: This peer-reviewed paper published in the Bentham Open journal presents evidence of unreacted thermitic material in the dust samples collected from the WTC site.
   - **Holder**: Bentham Open
   - **Catalog Reference**: DOI: 10.2174/1874125000902010004
   - **Verify at**: https://www.benthamopen.com/contents/pdf/1874-1250-2-1.pdf

2. **NIST NCSTAR 1 Report**: The NIST report on the WTC collapse does not address the thermitic materials found, which raises questions regarding the thoroughness of their investigation.
   - **Holder**: National Institute of Standards and Technology
   - **Catalog Reference**: NIST Report No. NCSTAR 1
   - **Verify at**: https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/NCSTAR/ncsar1.pdf

## Objection at full strength
NIST asserts that thermite residues can be found in various environments and do not necessarily indicate the use of explosives. They maintain that the presence of such materials could have resulted from construction debris or other benign sources.
