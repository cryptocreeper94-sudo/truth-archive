# C-0161 — Blue Frequency Reflectance Properties in Fire Survival

## The claim
Structures and vehicles with blue coloring demonstrated a higher survival rate during the Lahaina fire, indicating that blue frequency reflectance properties may play a role in resisting combustion under extreme heat conditions.

## The evidence
1. **Source S-0449**: Research paper published by the National Fire Protection Association on color and material performance during wildfires.
   - Catalog Number: NFPA-2023-003
   - Verification URL: https://www.nfpa.org/research/fire-color-2023

2. **Source S-0450**: Forensic photography documenting fire damage in Lahaina, including images of blue vehicles and roofs.
   - Catalog Number: FP-2023-001
   - Verification URL: https://www.firescience.gov/photos/2023-lahaina

## Objection at full strength
Skeptics highlight that the color of vehicles and roofs does not necessarily correlate with their material properties. They argue that the survival of blue items may simply be coincidental or due to other environmental factors, such as wind direction or the presence of fire breaks, rather than any intrinsic property related to color reflectance.
