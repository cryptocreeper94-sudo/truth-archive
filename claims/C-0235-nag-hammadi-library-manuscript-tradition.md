## The claim
The Nag Hammadi library, discovered in 1945, contains a collection of early Christian and Gnostic texts, including the Gospel of Thomas, which presents alternative views of Christian doctrine and was likely composed in the 2nd century CE.

## The evidence

## Objection at full strength
The authenticity and dating of the texts within the Nag Hammadi library are contested, as some scholars suggest they were written later than the proposed dates, and their Gnostic content raises questions about their acceptance within orthodox Christianity, leading to debates about their historical significance.
