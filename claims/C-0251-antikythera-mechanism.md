## The claim
The Antikythera mechanism, an ancient Greek analogue computer, is believed to have been used for astronomical calculations. Its complexity suggests that ancient civilizations possessed advanced technological knowledge that has been largely overlooked in mainstream archaeology. The mechanism's cataloged details raise questions about the acknowledgment of such technologies in historical narratives.

## Objection at full strength
Mainstream historians argue that the Antikythera mechanism is merely a sophisticated device for its time, with its functionality explained through the lens of established ancient technology. They assert that its complexity does not indicate an advanced civilization but rather reflects the ingenuity of Hellenistic engineering within its historical context.
