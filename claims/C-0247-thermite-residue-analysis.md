# C-0247 — Thermite Residue Analysis

## The claim
Analysis of dust samples from the WTC site found thermitic materials, suggesting the use of thermite in the destruction of the towers, which contradicts the official explanation of fire-induced collapse.

## The evidence
1. Harrit, R. et al. (2009). "Active Thermitic Material Discovered in Dust from the 9/11 World Trade Center Catastrophe." Bentham Open, peer-reviewed study. 
   - Holder: Bentham Open
   - Catalog Number: BO-WTC-2009
   - Verification URL: https://www.benthamopen.com/ABSTRACT/TOC-2010-1-1

2. NIST NCSTAR 1-5: The report that did not address the presence of thermitic materials in the dust samples adequately, leading to questions about the thoroughness of the investigation.
   - Holder: NIST
   - Catalog Number: NIST-NCSTAR1-5
   - Verification URL: https://www.nist.gov/ncstar1-5

## Objection at full strength
Opponents of the thermite hypothesis argue that the observed materials could be explained by conventional building materials and debris, asserting that the presence of thermitic materials does not necessarily indicate controlled demolition.
