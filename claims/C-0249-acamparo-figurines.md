## The claim
The Acámbaro figurines, discovered in Acámbaro, Mexico, are purported to depict various prehistoric creatures, suggesting the presence of ancient civilizations coexisting with dinosaurs. These artifacts have been cataloged and are held in various collections, raising questions about their true origins and the historical narratives surrounding them.

## Objection at full strength
Skeptics argue that the Acámbaro figurines are modern forgeries created for profit, pointing to the lack of consistent archaeological context and the dubious methods used in their discovery. Furthermore, many experts claim that the designs of the figurines can be linked to known artistic styles and cultural artifacts from the 20th century.
