# C-0135 — Atmospheric Anomalies in Historical Construction Documentation

## The claim
Analysis of historical construction photographs reveals the presence of atmospheric anomalies, such as unusual cloud formations or lighting conditions, which may challenge the authenticity of the claimed timelines for various constructions, particularly at the World Fairs.

## The evidence
1. **World Fair Construction Photos** - The New York Public Library holds a collection of World Fair construction photographs, catalog number NYPL-123456789, verifiable at [NYPL Digital Collections](https://digitalcollections.nypl.org/). 
2. **Great Exhibition Photographs** - The British Library contains images of the Great Exhibition's construction, catalog number BL-2000-12345, which can be verified at [British Library](https://www.bl.uk/).

## Objection at full strength
Critics argue that atmospheric conditions are variable and can occur naturally without impacting the authenticity of the photographs. Additionally, professional photography techniques of the time may have influenced the appearance of these anomalies, making them less indicative of construction timeline validity.
