# C-0208 — Cloud seeding programs by government contracts

## The claim
Various U.S. states, including Wyoming, have engaged in cloud seeding programs through government contracts aimed at increasing precipitation. These contracts detail methodologies and expected outcomes of weather modification activities.

## The evidence
- **Source**: Wyoming Water Resources Department cloud seeding project documentation
- **Holder**: State of Wyoming
- **Date of object**: 2020
- **Verification**: https://water.wyo.gov/CloudSeeding

## Objection at full strength
Opponents of cloud seeding argue that the effectiveness of such programs is not conclusively proven and that they could lead to unintended consequences such as altering local ecosystems or influencing weather patterns beyond targeted areas. Additionally, there are ethical concerns regarding the manipulation of natural weather systems.
