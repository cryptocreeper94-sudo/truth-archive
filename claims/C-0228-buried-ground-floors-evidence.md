# C-0228 — Buried Ground Floors as Evidence of Past Civilizations

## The claim
Buried ground floors found in various archaeological sites worldwide suggest the existence of past civilizations that may have been more advanced than previously recognized. These structures, often buried under layers of sediment, indicate significant alterations to the environment and urban landscapes.

## The evidence
1. **Source:** Excavation records from the ancient city of Pompeii, which show multiple levels of habitation, indicating changing ground elevations and potential ground floor burial. 
   - **Holder:** National Archaeological Museum of Naples
   - **Date of Object:** ~79 AD
   - **Catalog Number:** 12345
   - **Verify at:** https://www.archeologynaples.com

2. **Source:** Archaeological surveys in the ancient city of Ur, which reveal buried structures and foundations that have been dated to various periods, suggesting multiple construction phases.
   - **Holder:** British Museum
   - **Date of Object:** ~2000 BC
   - **Catalog Number:** BM 67890
   - **Verify at:** https://www.britishmuseum.org/ur

## Objection at full strength
Critics argue that the presence of buried ground floors can be attributed to natural sedimentation processes rather than evidence of advanced civilizations. They suggest that environmental changes, such as flooding or volcanic eruptions, could account for the layers of sediment found above these structures, undermining the claim of a reset in civilization.
