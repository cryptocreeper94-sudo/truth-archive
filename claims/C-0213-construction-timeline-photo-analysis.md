# C-0213 — Analysis of Construction Timeline Photos vs. Claimed Labor Availability for 19th-Century Structures

## The claim
This claim investigates discrepancies between construction timeline photographs of major 19th-century structures and the documented labor availability and claimed construction timelines. Specific attention is given to structures like the Brooklyn Bridge and the Eiffel Tower.

## The evidence
1. **Brooklyn Bridge Construction Photographs**: 
   - **Source**: Brooklyn Bridge Construction Archives
   - **Institution**: New York Public Library
   - **Catalog Number**: NYPL-BBC-001
   - **Verify at**: https://digitalcollections.nypl.org/items/nypl-bbc-001

2. **Eiffel Tower Construction Photographs**: 
   - **Source**: Eiffel Tower Historical Society
   - **Institution**: Bibliothèque nationale de France
   - **Catalog Number**: BNF-EIFF-001
   - **Verify at**: https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b53008227m/f1.item

## Objection at full strength
Critics argue that photographs can be staged or selectively chosen to represent a narrative that does not accurately reflect the entire construction process. Additionally, the availability of labor may have varied at different times, complicating any direct correlation to specific photographs.
