# C-0224 — Cloud seeding programs in the USA

## The claim
Various states in the USA have initiated cloud seeding programs, utilizing government contracts and specific methodologies to enhance precipitation.

## The evidence
- Source: Wyoming Weather Modification Program, State of Wyoming
  - Holder: Wyoming State Government
  - Catalog Number: WYP-CloudSeed-2023
  - Verify at: https://www.wyoming.gov/CloudSeeding

## Objection at full strength
Although cloud seeding programs exist, the efficacy and environmental impact of such interventions remain contested. Critics argue that cloud seeding does not guarantee increased rainfall and may carry ecological risks.
