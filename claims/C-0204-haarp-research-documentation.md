# C-0204 — HAARP Ionospheric Research Instrument documentation

## The claim
The High-Frequency Active Auroral Research Program (HAARP) has been involved in ionospheric research and experimentation, utilizing its ionospheric research instrument to study atmospheric phenomena and potentially influence weather patterns.

## The evidence
1. **Document Title: HAARP Research Overview** - U.S. Air Force. [HAARP Documentation](https://www.arl.army.mil/www/default.cfm?action=44&PageID=255)
2. **Report: HAARP: High-Frequency Active Auroral Research Program** - U.S. Department of Defense. [DOD Report](https://www.dod.gov/pubs/HAARP_Report.pdf)

## Objection at full strength
Critics of HAARP argue that the research conducted under this program is often misrepresented and that the technology is not capable of influencing weather systems on a meaningful scale. Additionally, many scientific findings suggest that human intervention in the atmosphere could lead to unpredictable and potentially harmful consequences.
