# C-0157 — Orphan Train CAS Records Availability

## The claim
CAS records related to the orphan train movement (1854-1929) are incomplete and not fully accessible, leading to gaps in understanding the historical impact of these programs on the children involved.

## The evidence
1. A report from the New York Foundling Hospital indicates that many records were lost or destroyed over time, with some records only being available to specific researchers or under limited conditions.
2. The New York State Archives holds records related to the orphan train movement, but many are not digitized or cataloged for public access, making it difficult to obtain comprehensive data.

## Objection at full strength
Opponents may argue that existing CAS records are sufficient for historical research and that the archives provide a comprehensive overview of the orphan train movement. However, the existence of lost records and restricted access indicates gaps in documentation that hinder full understanding.
