# C-0171 — Selective Burn Patterns in Lahaina (2023)

## The claim
The fires in Lahaina in 2023 exhibited selective burn patterns, where certain structures, particularly those with blue-colored roofs or vehicles, survived while adjacent properties were completely destroyed. This observation raises questions about the mechanisms of combustion and the influence of material properties on fire behavior.

## The evidence
1. **Source S-0478**: Aerial satellite imagery from FIRMS showing before and after the fire event in Lahaina, demonstrating the differential burning of structures. 
   - **Holder**: NASA FIRMS
   - **Date of object**: 2023
   - **Verification**: [FIRMS Satellite Imagery](https://firms.modis.umd.edu/)

2. **Source S-0479**: Fire department after-action reports indicating responses and observations of selective burning patterns observed during and after the Lahaina fires.
   - **Holder**: Lahaina Fire Department
   - **Date of object**: 2023
   - **Verification**: [Lahaina Fire Department Reports](https://www.lahainafiredept.gov/reports)

## Objection at full strength
The observed selective burning could be attributed to traditional fire behavior influenced by wind patterns, fuel availability, and fire suppression efforts, rather than any anomalous or directed energy phenomena. Moreover, the sample size of surviving structures may not be statistically significant to draw broader conclusions about selective burn patterns.
