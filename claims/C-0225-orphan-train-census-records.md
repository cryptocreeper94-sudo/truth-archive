# C-0225 — Orphan Train Census Records Gaps

## The claim
Census records from the period of the Orphan Train movement (1854-1929) exhibit significant gaps in documentation, particularly for children who were relocated through these programs. Many children who were transported to new homes do not appear on local census records, raising questions about their legal status and the completeness of historical records.

## The evidence
- **Source S-0655**: 1880 Census records, available at the National Archives, which show incomplete entries for certain counties known to be involved in the Orphan Train movement. 
- **Source S-0656**: Documentation from the New York Foundling Hospital, which includes records of children sent out on trains but lacks corresponding census entries for many of these children.

## Objection at full strength
Census records can be incomplete due to various reasons, such as errors in recording, loss of records, or discrepancies in reporting by guardians. Additionally, local census practices may have varied, leading to inconsistencies in how children were documented.
