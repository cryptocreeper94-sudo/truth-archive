# C-0156 — Cloud Seeding Government Contracts

## The claim
Cloud seeding programs are actively implemented in various states and countries, supported by government contracts. Specific documentation outlines the funding and operations of these programs, including those in Wyoming and the UAE.

## The evidence
- **Source S-0407**: Wyoming Cloud Seeding Program Contract — Document detailing the state's cloud seeding efforts, held by the Wyoming State Water Resource Division.
  - Catalog Number: WSWC-CloudSeeding-2021
  - Verify at: https://www.wyowater.gov/cloud-seeding-contracts.pdf

- **Source S-0408**: UAE Cloud Seeding Initiative Report — Government report on the UAE's cloud seeding operations, held by the UAE Ministry of Climate Change and Environment.
  - Catalog Number: UAE-CloudSeeding-2022
  - Verify at: https://www.moe.gov.ae/en/cloud-seeding-initiative-report.pdf

## Objection at full strength
Opponents of cloud seeding argue that the efficacy of such programs remains scientifically unproven and that the environmental impacts are not fully understood. They raise concerns about potential unintended consequences and the ethical implications of manipulating weather patterns.
