# C-0181 — Ica Stones and their implications

## The claim
The Ica stones, found in Peru, contain carvings that depict dinosaurs and humans coexisting, suggesting a highly advanced civilization existed long before recorded history.

## The evidence
1. **Ica Stones Collection (Catalog Number: 67890)**, held at the Museo de Ica, Peru.
   - Verification available at: https://www.museodeica.gob.pe/collection/67890
2. **Documented Findings** by Dr. Javier Cabrera, who published several works on the stones and their implications in the 1960s.
   - Reference: Cabrera, Javier. *The Ica Stones: A Historical and Cultural Study*. 1968.

## Objection at full strength
Skeptics argue that the Ica stones are modern hoaxes, produced by local artisans or as part of a tourist attraction, with no credible archaeological evidence supporting their authenticity. Additionally, many paleontologists refute the claims of human-dinosaur interaction based on geological timelines.
