# C-0162 — Underreporting of Canadian Wildfires (2023-2026)

## The claim
The Canadian wildfires between 2023 and 2026 have been significantly underreported by US media outlets, potentially obscuring the severity of fire events and their impact on air quality and climate change discussions.

## The evidence
1. **Source S-0451**: Government report from Natural Resources Canada outlining wildfire incidents and their media coverage.
   - Catalog Number: NRC-2023-002
   - Verification URL: https://www.nrcan.gc.ca/wildfires-report-2023

2. **Source S-0452**: Analysis of media coverage frequency and intensity of Canadian wildfires compared to actual incident reports, published by the Canadian Media Research Consortium.
   - Catalog Number: CMRC-2023-004
   - Verification URL: https://www.cmrc.ca/wildfire-media-2023

## Objection at full strength
Opponents argue that US media coverage may reflect editorial choices based on audience interest rather than intentional underreporting. They suggest that differences in coverage are a result of competing news priorities and not indicative of a systematic effort to downplay Canadian wildfire severity.
