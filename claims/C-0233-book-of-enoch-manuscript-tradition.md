## The claim
The Book of Enoch has a complex manuscript tradition, with significant versions found among the Dead Sea Scrolls, notably the Ethiopic and Slavonic texts, dating back to at least the 3rd century BCE.

## Objection at full strength
The authenticity of the Book of Enoch is contested; several scholars argue that it was written in the first century CE, casting doubt on its ancient origins. Moreover, some Christian traditions classify it as apocryphal, implying its exclusion from canonical texts may be justified based on theological grounds.
