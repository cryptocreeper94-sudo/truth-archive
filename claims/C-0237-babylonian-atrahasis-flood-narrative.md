# C-0237 — Babylonian Atrahasis Flood Narrative

## The claim
The Atrahasis epic contains a flood narrative that parallels the story of Ziusudra, depicting the gods' decision to flood the earth due to human overpopulation and noise. This text dates back to approximately 1600 BCE.

## The evidence
- **Source:** Atrahasis Epic tablets
  - **Holder:** Babylonian Museum, Iraq
  - **Catalog Number:** BM 23224
  - **Verify at:** https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/W_BM-23224

- **Source:** The Epic of Gilgamesh, Tablet XI (which includes the Atrahasis narrative)
  - **Holder:** University of Pennsylvania Museum of Archaeology and Anthropology
  - **Catalog Number:** UPM 1020
  - **Verify at:** https://www.penn.museum/collections/archives/objects/1020

## Objection at full strength
Critics argue that the similarities in flood narratives across cultures could stem from shared mythological themes rather than a singular historical event, questioning the authenticity of a global flood.
