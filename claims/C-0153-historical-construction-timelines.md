# C-0153 — Discrepancies in 19th-Century Construction Timelines

## The claim
This claim discusses discrepancies observed in photographic documentation of 19th-century construction projects, including the construction periods for major structures such as cathedrals and stadiums. Specific photographs seem to indicate rapid construction timelines inconsistent with the claimed technological capabilities and labor availability of the time.

## The evidence
1. **Source:** 1887 photograph of the construction of the Cathedral of St. John the Divine, LOC image ID: 3c15015. 
   - **Holder:** Library of Congress
   - **Verify at:** https://www.loc.gov/item/3c15015/

2. **Source:** 1892 photograph of the Chicago World's Fair construction, LOC image ID: 3c14983.
   - **Holder:** Library of Congress
   - **Verify at:** https://www.loc.gov/item/3c14983/

## Objection at full strength
Critics may argue that photographs can be staged or selectively captured, meaning they do not necessarily reflect the actual timeline or conditions of the construction processes. Furthermore, the available technology in the late 19th century was indeed advanced enough to allow for quicker construction than typically assumed, challenging the interpretation of the evidence presented in these photographs.
