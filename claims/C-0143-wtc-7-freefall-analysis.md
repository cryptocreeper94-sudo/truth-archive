# C-0143 — WTC 7 Freefall Analysis

## The claim
The freefall of WTC 7 for approximately 2.25 seconds during its collapse, as analyzed in the University of Alaska Fairbanks study (Hulsey 2020), indicates that the building's structure was compromised in a manner not fully explained by official narratives, particularly regarding the role of office fires.

## The evidence
1. Hulsey, J. (2020). "Final Report on the Collapse of World Trade Center Building 7". University of Alaska Fairbanks.
2. FEMA BPAT Report Appendix C, which discusses structural failures and mentions sulfidation and intergranular melting of steel.

## Objection at full strength
Critics argue that the freefall duration does not necessarily indicate controlled demolition but could result from the progressive collapse of the building due to fire-induced damage, which is supported by other structural analyses and investigations.
