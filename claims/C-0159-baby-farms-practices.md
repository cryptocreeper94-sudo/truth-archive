# C-0159 — Historical Practices of Baby Farms

## The claim
Records of baby farms in the 19th century show a pattern of neglect and abuse, which are not fully documented in historical accounts, leading to a distorted understanding of their operations.

## The evidence
1. Reports from the British Society for the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals (SPCA) detail various instances of neglect and abuse within baby farms, though many specific cases were never formally recorded.
2. The National Archives of Ireland contains records that suggest many children placed in baby farms were never adopted or properly cared for.

## Objection at full strength
Some may argue that the existing accounts and reports provide a sufficient overview of baby farm operations. However, the lack of comprehensive documentation indicates that many cases remain hidden from historical scrutiny.
