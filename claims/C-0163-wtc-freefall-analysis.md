# C-0163 — WTC 1/2/7 Freefall Analysis

## The claim
The FEMA BPAT Report Appendix C indicates that the sulfidation and intergranular melting of steel found in the debris of WTC 1 and 2 cannot be explained by typical office fires, suggesting alternative heat sources may have been involved in the collapse of these buildings. Furthermore, WTC 7 exhibited freefall for approximately 2.5 seconds, which is indicative of a complete failure of the structural supports beneath it.

## The evidence
1. FEMA BPAT Report Appendix C, available at the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) under FOIA document number NIST FOIA 2008-252.
2. University of Alaska Fairbanks Finite Element Study by Hulsey et al. (2020) indicates discrepancies in the official narrative regarding the collapse mechanisms of WTC 7. Available at [UAF](https://www.uaf.edu).

## Objection at full strength
Opponents argue that the NIST investigation provides a thorough analysis of the collapse mechanisms for WTC 1, 2, and 7, attributing the failures to fire and structural damage, thus discounting the need for alternative explanations or additional evidence.
