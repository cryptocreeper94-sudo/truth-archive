# C-0258 — Thermite Residue Analysis in WTC Collapse

## The claim
Analysis of thermite residues in the dust samples collected from Ground Zero indicates the presence of materials consistent with thermitic reactions, suggesting the possibility of controlled demolition. Harrit et al. (2009) published a peer-reviewed paper in Bentham Open that discusses the implications of these findings on the official narrative regarding the causes of the collapse. 

## The evidence
- Harrit, N., et al. (2009). Active Thermitic Material Discovered in Dust from the 9/11 World Trade Center Catastrophe. Bentham Open, DOI: 10.2174/1874225000902010003.
- Sample analysis reports from the Department of Civil Engineering, University of Alaska Fairbanks, project number WTC-DUST-2009.

## Objection at full strength
Skeptics argue that the presence of thermitic materials in dust does not necessarily indicate the use of such materials in a demolition context. They contend that the observed residues could arise from other sources, including the high-temperature fires and chemical reactions associated with the building collapse, and that the conclusions drawn are overly simplistic.
