# C-0238 — Hebrew Name Meanings and Their Etymology

## The claim
The meanings of Hebrew names often reflect attributes, events, or aspirations linked to the biblical narratives. For example, the name 'Abraham' is derived from 'Avram,' meaning 'exalted father,' later transformed to mean 'father of many nations' in Genesis. This suggests that Hebrew names encapsulate genealogical and cultural significance.

## The evidence
- **Source**: The Hebrew Bible (Tanakh) 
  - **Holder**: Various editions, including the Aleppo Codex
  - **Date of Object**: ~10th century CE
  - **Verification**: Available at [The British Library](https://www.bl.uk/collection-items/the-aleppo-codex)

## Objection at full strength
The argument that Hebrew names carry specific meanings can be contested by the fact that name meanings can vary based on regional dialects and historical contexts. Additionally, modern interpretations may not accurately reflect ancient understandings, making it difficult to establish definitive meanings.
