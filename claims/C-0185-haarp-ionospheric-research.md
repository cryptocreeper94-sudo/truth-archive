# C-0185 — HAARP Ionospheric Research Instrument Documentation

## The claim
The High-Frequency Active Auroral Research Program (HAARP) is a research program that studies the ionosphere using powerful radio frequency transmissions. Documentation reveals its potential applications in atmospheric research and manipulation.

## The evidence
- **S-0535** — HAARP Research Program overview document, catalog number: HAARP-2021-Research, verify-at: https://www.haarp.alaska.edu/
- **S-0536** — U.S. Air Force report on HAARP, catalog number: USAF-HAARP-2020, verify-at: https://www.af.mil/HAARP/overview

## Objection at full strength
Critics of HAARP raise concerns about the potential for misuse in weather modification and military applications. They also argue that the environmental impacts of such high-energy transmissions on the ionosphere are not fully understood, leading to calls for more stringent oversight.
