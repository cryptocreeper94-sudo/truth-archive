# C-0165 — Shanksville Debris Field Distribution

## The claim
The distribution of debris at the Shanksville crash site of Flight 93 shows an anomalous pattern that does not correspond to a typical aircraft crash, suggesting that the debris field was manipulated or resulted from an alternate cause.

## The evidence
1. National Transportation Safety Board (NTSB) Investigation Report (2001), available under FOIA document number NTSB FOIA 2002-001.
2. Analysis published in the Proceedings of the International Society of the 9/11 Studies, available at [IS911Studies](https://www.is911studies.com).

## Objection at full strength
Skeptics of this claim assert that the debris distribution can be explained by the nature of the crash and the terrain, maintaining that eyewitness accounts of the crash and recovery operations align with the official narrative.
