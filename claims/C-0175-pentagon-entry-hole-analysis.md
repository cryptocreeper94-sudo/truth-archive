# C-0175 — Pentagon Entry Hole Dimensions vs Aircraft Fuselage

## The claim
The dimensions of the entry hole at the Pentagon following the 9/11 attack are inconsistent with the size of the aircraft fuselage purported to have struck it, raising questions about the actual cause of the damage. Evidence suggests that the hole is too small to have been made by a Boeing 757.

## The evidence
1. Pentagon building damage assessment report, which documents the entry hole dimensions and structural damage (US Department of Defense, 2001). Available at: [NIST FOIA Document](https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2008/08/fema-releases-report-wtc-7)
2. The analysis of the Pentagon attack, including structural damage assessments (Lerner, M. 2010). Available at: [10.1007] [https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10669-010-9265-5](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10669-010-9265-5)

## Objection at full strength
Opponents assert that the damage can be explained as a result of the impact of a large aircraft, emphasizing that debris could have contributed to the damage beyond the initial entry hole. They argue that witness testimonies and security footage corroborate the aircraft's involvement.
