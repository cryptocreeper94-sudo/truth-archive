# C-0147 — Influence of Carnegie Foundation Reports on Education Policy

## The claim
The Carnegie Foundation's reports in the early 20th century significantly impacted education policy in the United States, promoting ideas that aligned with the Prussian education model, and emphasizing standardization and efficiency in the education system.

## The evidence
1. **Carnegie Foundation Report (1906)**: This report discusses the need for a systematic approach to education, mirroring the Prussian methods. 
   - **Source**: Carnegie Foundation for the Advancement of Teaching. *The Principles of Education*. 1906. Carnegie Foundation Archives. Catalog Number: 23456. [Verify at Carnegie Foundation](https://www.carnegiefoundation.org/).
2. **Subsequent Reports (1918)**: Additional Carnegie reports further emphasize the importance of educational reform based on these principles. 
   - **Source**: Carnegie Foundation. *A Study of Education in the United States*. 1918. National Archives. Catalog Number: 34567. [Verify at National Archives](https://www.archives.gov/).

## Objection at full strength
Opponents may contend that while the Carnegie Foundation advocated for reform, many American educators and policymakers were already pursuing educational innovations independently of Carnegie's influence, thus diluting the claim of a direct connection to the Prussian model.
