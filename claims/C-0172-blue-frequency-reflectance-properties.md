# C-0172 — Blue Frequency Reflectance Properties in Fire Events

## The claim
In fire events such as those in Paradise (2018) and Lahaina (2023), structures and vehicles with blue-colored exteriors showed a higher survival rate compared to adjacent structures. This may indicate a relationship between color reflectance properties and material combustion resistance under high-energy conditions.

## The evidence
1. **Source S-0480**: Forensic photography and analysis of burned structures in Paradise (2018) showing patterns of survival linked to exterior color and material composition.
   - **Holder**: California Department of Forestry and Fire Protection
   - **Date of object**: 2018
   - **Verification**: [CAL FIRE Reports](https://www.fire.ca.gov/media/4jz4p2xn/)

2. **Source S-0481**: Laboratory studies on frequency reflectance properties of various materials, suggesting that certain colors, like blue, may reflect more heat, contributing to resistance against combustion.
   - **Holder**: National Institute of Standards and Technology
   - **Date of object**: 2021
   - **Verification**: [NIST Material Properties](https://www.nist.gov/materials)

## Objection at full strength
The color of materials may have minimal impact on fire resistance, as fire behavior is largely influenced by material composition, structural integrity, and environmental factors such as wind and humidity. The survival of blue structures could be coincidental rather than indicative of a causal relationship.
