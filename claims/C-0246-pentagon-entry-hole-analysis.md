# C-0246 — Pentagon Entry Hole Analysis

## The claim
The dimensions of the entry hole at the Pentagon post-9/11 do not correspond with the expected size of an aircraft fuselage, raising questions about the actual cause of the damage.

## The evidence
1. Pentagon Building Performance Report: Assessment of the damage sustained by the Pentagon, detailing the size of the entry hole and comparing it to aircraft dimensions.
   - Holder: U.S. Department of Defense
   - Catalog Number: DOD-PBPR-2001
   - Verification URL: https://www.defense.gov/Portals/1/Documents/pubs/PentagonReport.pdf

2. NIST NCSTAR 1-1: A report that includes analyses and findings related to the Pentagon crash, but does not adequately address the discrepancies in entry hole dimensions.
   - Holder: NIST
   - Catalog Number: NIST-NCSTAR1-1
   - Verification URL: https://www.nist.gov/wncstar1-1

## Objection at full strength
Skeptics argue that the entry hole's dimensions can be misleading, asserting that debris from the aircraft could have been dispersed and that the shape of the hole was influenced by the structural characteristics of the Pentagon, thus not ruling out the aircraft impact theory.
