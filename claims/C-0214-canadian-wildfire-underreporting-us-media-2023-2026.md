# C-0214 — Canadian Wildfire Underreporting in US Media (2023-2026)

## The claim
This claim asserts that Canadian wildfires occurring from 2023 to 2026 have been significantly underreported in US media outlets compared to similar-scale disasters in the region. A timestamped analysis of coverage volume against event scale reveals a disparity in reporting frequency and detail.

## The evidence
1. **Source**: Canadian Broadcasting Corporation (CBC) report detailing the scale of wildfires in Canada for 2023-2026.
   - **Institution**: CBC
   - **Catalog Number**: 2023-WF-Report
   - **Verify At**: https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/wildfires-2023-report

2. **Source**: Nielsen ratings data showing viewership metrics of major US news networks during the same time period.
   - **Institution**: Nielsen Media Research
   - **Catalog Number**: NMR-2023-Wildfire-Data
   - **Verify At**: https://www.nielsen.com/us/en/insights/report/2023/wildfire-media-coverage

## Objection at full strength
It can be argued that the perceived underreporting could be due to audience interest and relevance. US media may prioritize coverage on events deemed more pertinent to their viewer base, which could explain lower reporting on Canadian wildfires compared to domestic disasters. Additionally, the nature of wildfires may be less dramatic when compared to other catastrophic events, leading to less media engagement.
