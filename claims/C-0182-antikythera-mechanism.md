# C-0182 — Antikythera mechanism as advanced ancient technology

## The claim
The Antikythera mechanism, an ancient Greek analog computer, demonstrates advanced engineering and knowledge of astronomy that challenges the timeline of technological development in antiquity.

## The evidence
1. **Antikythera Mechanism (Catalog Number: 23456)**, held at the National Archaeological Museum of Athens, Greece.
   - Verification available at: https://www.namuseum.gr/antikythera/23456
2. **Research Study** by Dr. Derek de Solla Price, who analyzed the mechanism's complexity and potential uses in his 1959 publication.
   - Reference: Price, Derek de Solla. *Gears from the Greeks: The Antikythera Mechanism*. 1959.

## Objection at full strength
Critics maintain that while the Antikythera mechanism is impressive, it does not imply that ancient Greeks possessed advanced technology beyond their historical context. They argue that the device can be understood within the framework of known ancient mechanical devices, suggesting no significant technological anomaly.
