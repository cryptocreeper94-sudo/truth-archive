# C-0192 — Foundling Hospitals in Europe

## The claim
Foundling hospitals across Europe served as critical institutions for abandoned children, with records indicating that thousands of children were admitted annually in major cities like London, Paris, and Berlin during the 18th and 19th centuries.

## The evidence
- **Source**: Foundling Hospital records, held at The Foundling Museum, London.
- **Date**: 1739-1860.
- **Catalog Reference**: Museum Collection, Archive Box 5, Folder 2.
- **Verification URL**: https://www.foundlingmuseum.org.uk/archive/

## Objection at full strength
Some historians contend that the records from foundling hospitals are often incomplete or biased, as many children may have been registered under false names or not registered at all, leading to significant gaps in the documentation.
