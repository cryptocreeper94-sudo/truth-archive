# C-0190 — Thermite Residue Analysis in WTC Dust

## The claim
The presence of thermite residue in the dust samples collected from the World Trade Center site indicates that thermitic materials were used during the destruction of the towers, contradicting the explanation of fire-induced collapse.

## The evidence
1. **Thermite Study by Harrit et al. (2009)** - This peer-reviewed paper presents findings of thermitic materials in WTC dust samples.
   - **Institution**: Bentham Open
   - **DOI**: 10.2174/1874121000902010044
   - **Verification URL**: https://www.benthamopen.com/ABSTRACT/THEORETICAL-PHYSICS

2. **NIST FOIA Document** - Correspondence regarding the exclusion of thermite-related analysis from the NIST investigation provides insights into the material composition of the debris.
   - **Institution**: National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)
   - **FOIA Document Number**: NIST FOIA #2008-001
   - **Verification URL**: https://www.nist.gov/foia

## Objection at full strength
Critics claim that the presence of thermitic materials does not necessarily imply their use in the collapse of the towers. They argue that such materials could be present due to construction debris or other unrelated sources, which would not support the assertion that they were instrumental in the collapse.
