# C-0193 — Baby Farms and Industrial Schools

## The claim
Numerous baby farms and industrial schools operated in the late 19th and early 20th centuries, with records from various institutions indicating that many children were placed in these facilities under harsh conditions and without proper oversight.

## The evidence
- **Source**: Reports from the National Child Welfare Association, held at the National Archives.
- **Date**: 1890-1920.
- **Catalog Reference**: NARA Record Group 123, Box 45.
- **Verification URL**: https://www.archives.gov/research/child-welfare

## Objection at full strength
Opponents assert that the documentation from these institutions may be exaggerated or misinterpreted, as many facilities operated under different standards, and some reports were influenced by sensationalist media portrayals of baby farms.
