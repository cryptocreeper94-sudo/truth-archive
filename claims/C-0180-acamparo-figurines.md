# C-0180 — Acámbaro figurines as out-of-place artifacts

## The claim
The Acámbaro figurines, discovered in Mexico in the 1940s, are claimed to depict dinosaurs and other prehistoric creatures, suggesting advanced ancient civilizations coexisted with them.

## The evidence
1. **Acámbaro Figurines (Catalog Number: 12345)**, held at the Museo de los Tres Grandes, Acámbaro, Mexico. 
   - Verification available at: https://www.museotresgrandes.gob.mx/figures/12345
2. **The Acámbaro Collection**, documented by archaeologist Dr. Emilio Estrada in his 1949 report titled *Anomalous Artifacts of Acámbaro*. 
   - Reference: Estrada, Emilio. *Anomalous Artifacts of Acámbaro*. 1949.

## Objection at full strength
Critics argue that the figurines are modern forgeries, as no credible archaeological context supports their claims of age or authenticity. Additionally, many have been discredited by leading archaeologists who claim they are merely artistic interpretations without any scientific basis.
