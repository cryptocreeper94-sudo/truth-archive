# C-0176 — Shanksville Debris Field Distribution

## The claim
The distribution of debris in the Shanksville crash site of Flight 93 is inconsistent with an aircraft crash. The debris field is remarkably small and concentrated, which raises questions about the nature of the crash and suggests alternative explanations beyond a commercial aircraft impact.

## The evidence
1. National Transportation Safety Board report on Flight 93 crash site, which details debris distribution and recovery operations (NTSB, 2002). Available at: [NIST FOIA Document](https://www.ntsb.gov/investigations/2002/pennsylvania/95/93.html)
2. Forensic analysis of the crash site, which discusses the unique characteristics of the debris field (Baker, A. 2004). Available at: [10.1111] [https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2210675070000153](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2210675070000153)

## Objection at full strength
Critics argue that the debris field size can be attributed to the impact dynamics of a high-speed crash into soft ground, which could result in a more localized debris pattern. They also cite witness accounts of an aircraft crash corroborating the official narrative.
