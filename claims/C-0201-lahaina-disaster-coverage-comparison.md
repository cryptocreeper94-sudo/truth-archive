# C-0201 — Coverage of Lahaina Disaster Compared to Other Disasters

## The claim
This claim examines the media coverage of the Lahaina disaster in Hawaii in comparison to other significant disasters that occurred around the same time, highlighting discrepancies in coverage volume and audience engagement metrics.

## Objection at full strength
Media outlets may argue that the high volume of coverage for the Lahaina disaster is justified by the local impact and the unprecedented nature of the event, thus not necessarily indicating a lack of coverage for other disasters. They may also point to audience ratings and engagement data as evidence of public interest driving coverage decisions.
