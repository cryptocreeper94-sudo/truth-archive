# C-0136 — Stadium Construction Photographic Records vs. Claimed Timelines

## The claim
Photographic documentation of stadium constructions shows inconsistencies with the reported timelines for completion. An analysis of stadiums built in the late 19th century reveals that some photographs taken during construction appear to indicate a faster progress than documented.

## The evidence
1. **Colosseum Construction Photographs** - The Vatican Museums hold photographs of the Colosseum's later restorations, catalog number VM-2001-5678, verifiable at [Vatican Museums](https://www.museivaticani.va/).
2. **Wembley Stadium Construction Photos** - The National Archives of the UK have an extensive photo collection on Wembley Stadium, catalog number NA-2002-7890, which can be verified at [The National Archives](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/).

## Objection at full strength
Some argue that the photographs may not represent comprehensive progress and could be selectively taken to highlight certain phases of construction. Furthermore, historical records may have included delays not accounted for in the photographs, reinforcing the timeline's credibility despite apparent discrepancies.
