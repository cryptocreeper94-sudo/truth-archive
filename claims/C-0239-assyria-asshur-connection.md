# C-0239 — The Connection between Assyria and Asshur

## The claim
The ancient region of Assyria is believed to derive its name from the city of Asshur, which was named after the deity Asshur, the principal god of the Assyrian empire. This connection suggests that the cultural and religious practices of the Assyrians were deeply intertwined with their identity as a people.

## The evidence
- **Source**: The Assyrian Empire: A Historical Overview
  - **Holder**: The Metropolitan Museum of Art
  - **Date of Object**: 1980
  - **Verification**: Available at [The Metropolitan Museum of Art](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/325685)

## Objection at full strength
Some scholars argue that the connection between Assyria and Asshur is tenuous and not universally accepted. There are alternative theories regarding the origin of the name Assyria, suggesting it may not have a direct link to the city of Asshur but rather to other geographic or linguistic roots.
