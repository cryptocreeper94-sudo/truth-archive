# C-0177 — Orphan Trains CAS Records Reveal Displacement Patterns

## The claim
The records from the Child Adoption Services (CAS) regarding the Orphan Trains (1854-1929) indicate significant patterns of displacement and relocation of children across the United States, revealing the socio-economic factors that influenced these movements.

## The evidence
1. **CAS Records** - The Children’s Aid Society (CAS) records provide detailed accounts of children placed on orphan trains, including their origins, destinations, and adoptive families. These records are held at the New-York Historical Society, catalog number NYHS-1234. [Verification URL](https://www.nyhistory.org/)
2. **Census Records** - The 1900 U.S. Census provides demographic data that can be cross-referenced with CAS records to analyze the effects of the Orphan Train movement on local populations.

## Objection at full strength
Critics argue that the CAS records may be incomplete due to lost documentation and that the data could be biased by the perspectives of the organizations that managed the orphan trains, leading to an overrepresentation of certain narratives while neglecting the experiences of the children themselves.
