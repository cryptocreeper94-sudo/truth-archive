# C-0259 — WTC 7 Freefall Analysis

## The claim
The analysis of WTC 7's collapse revealed a period of freefall lasting approximately 2.25 seconds, which indicates an absence of structural resistance during this time. This phenomenon cannot be explained by the official narrative that attributes the collapse solely to office fires. The FEMA BPAT Report Appendix C discusses the sulfidation and intergranular melting of steel, which was not adequately addressed in the NIST reports.

## The evidence
1. FEMA BPAT Report, Appendix C, which discusses the observed steel deformation and the lack of structural integrity. 
2. Hulsey, J. (2020). “Final Report on the Collapse of World Trade Center 7” from the University of Alaska Fairbanks.
   - Holder: University of Alaska Fairbanks
   - Catalog Reference: UAF-WTC7-2020
   - Verify at: https://www.uaf.edu/wtc7/

## Objection at full strength
The NIST report claims that the collapse of WTC 7 was due to fire-induced damage, stating that the freefall period lasted only a short time and that the building's structural elements were still engaged. Critics argue that this view does not account for the observed freefall duration and the significant steel melting that was documented.
