# C-0158 — Incomplete Records of European Foundling Hospitals

## The claim
Foundling hospitals across Europe often have incomplete or poorly maintained records, making comprehensive research on their operations and the children they served difficult.

## The evidence
1. A study conducted on the records of the Foundling Hospital in London indicates significant gaps and discrepancies in the documentation of admissions and discharges.
2. The Archives of the French Foundling Hospital reveal that many records were lost during the French Revolution and are thus unavailable for contemporary research.

## Objection at full strength
While some may argue that the surviving records are adequate for research, the significant gaps, especially during tumultuous periods, highlight the incomplete nature of the historical record.
