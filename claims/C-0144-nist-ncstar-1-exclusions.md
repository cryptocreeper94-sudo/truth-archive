# C-0144 — NIST NCSTAR 1 Exclusions of Evidence

## The claim
The NIST NCSTAR 1 report has been criticized for excluding critical evidence related to sulfidation and intergranular melting of steel found in the debris of WTC 1, 2, and 7, which cannot be solely attributed to office fires, raising questions about the completeness of the investigation.

## The evidence
1. NIST NCSTAR 1 Report, National Institute of Standards and Technology, which details the investigation but omits discussion on specific findings of steel melting.
2. FEMA BPAT Report Appendix C, which documents observations of melted steel and its implications for structural integrity.

## Objection at full strength
Defenders of the NIST report assert that the investigation's methodology was thorough and that the findings related to steel melting were either misinterpreted or overstated by critics, as the results align with known behaviors of steel under fire conditions.
