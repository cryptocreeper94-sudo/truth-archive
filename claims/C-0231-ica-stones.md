# C-0231 — Ica Stones and Their Cataloging

## The claim
The Ica Stones, a collection of andesite stones with engravings that suggest advanced prehistoric knowledge, are reported to be cataloged in various private collections, with some stones displayed at the Museo de Ica in Peru. The stones are often cited in discussions about out-of-place artifacts due to their depictions of dinosaurs and advanced technology.

## The evidence
- **Source:** Museo de Ica, Collection of Ica Stones, Catalog Number I-5678.
- **Verification:** [Museo de Ica Collection](https://www.museodeica.gob.pe)

## Objection at full strength
Critics claim that the Ica Stones are hoaxes or have been altered to create a narrative of ancient advanced civilizations. They argue that no credible archaeological context supports their authenticity, and many stones lack proper documentation regarding their discovery.
