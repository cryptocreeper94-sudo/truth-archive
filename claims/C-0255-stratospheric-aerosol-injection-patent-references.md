# C-0255 — Stratospheric Aerosol Injection Patent References

## The claim
Stratospheric aerosol injection (SAI) has been proposed as a method to combat climate change, with several patents filed related to the technology. Notable patents include US Patent 5003186, which discusses methods for altering atmospheric conditions.

## The evidence
- **Source S-0709**: US Patent 5003186 discusses stratospheric aerosol injection methods. It is held at the United States Patent and Trademark Office (USPTO) under catalog number 5003186. [USPTO link](https://patft.uspto.gov/netacgi/nph-Parser?Sect1=PTO1&Sect2=HITOFF&d=PALL&p=1&u=%2Fnetahtml%2FPTO%2Fsrchnum.htm&r=1&f=G&l=50&s1=5003186.PN.&OS=PN/5003186&RS=PN/5003186).

## Objection at full strength
Critics argue that the effectiveness and safety of SAI are unproven and could have unintended consequences on global weather patterns and ecosystems, referencing studies that highlight potential risks associated with geoengineering technologies.
