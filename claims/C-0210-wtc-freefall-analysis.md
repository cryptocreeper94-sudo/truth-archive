# C-0210 — WTC 1/2/7 Freefall Analysis and FEMA Findings

## The claim
The FEMA BPAT Report Appendix C indicates that the observed freefall acceleration of WTC 1, 2, and 7 cannot be explained by the heat from office fires alone, as it notes sulfidation and intergranular melting of steel at temperatures not achievable by such fires. This suggests additional factors contributing to the collapse.

## The evidence
- FEMA BPAT Report Appendix C, held by the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), catalog number NIST NCSTAR 1-6, accessible via [NIST](https://www.nist.gov/publications/final-report-national-construction-safety-team-ntst-investigation-collapse-world-trade-center)
- University of Alaska Fairbanks WTC7 finite element study by Hulsey (2020), hosted at University of Alaska Fairbanks, accessible via [UAF](https://www.uaf.edu/)

## Objection at full strength
The NIST NCSTAR 1 report contends that the observed freefall is a result of the gravitational acceleration and does not imply controlled demolition. Critics argue that the methodology used by FEMA lacks sufficient rigor and that NIST has adequately addressed the thermal conditions leading to the collapse without the need for additional explanations.
