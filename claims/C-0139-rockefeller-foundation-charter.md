# C-0139 — Rockefeller Foundation Charter and Its Global Health Initiatives

## The claim
The charter of the Rockefeller Foundation, established in 1913, outlines its mission to promote the well-being of humanity through health, education, and agricultural initiatives. The foundation has played a significant role in global health efforts, especially in combating diseases and improving health systems worldwide.

## The evidence
- **Source:** Rockefeller Foundation Charter
- **Holder:** Rockefeller Foundation
- **Date of Object:** 1913
- **Verify at:** https://www.rockefellerfoundation.org/about/charter/

## Objection at full strength
Opponents often criticize the Rockefeller Foundation for its historical involvement in controversial health policies, including eugenics and population control measures. This has led to skepticism regarding the foundation's current initiatives and whether they genuinely promote public health or serve specific political agendas.
