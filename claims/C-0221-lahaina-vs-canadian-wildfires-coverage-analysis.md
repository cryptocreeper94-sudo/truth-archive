# C-0221 — Lahaina vs Canadian Wildfires Coverage Analysis

## The claim
This claim analyzes the disparity in media coverage between the Lahaina wildfires and Canadian wildfires from 2023 to 2026. Despite the comparable scale of destruction, Lahaina received significantly more coverage in US media compared to the Canadian wildfires, suggesting a pattern of selective reporting based on geographic and cultural factors.

## The evidence
1. **Source:** Archived News Articles and Broadcast Transcripts on Lahaina Wildfires, 2023.
   - **Holder:** National Archives and Records Administration.
   - **Catalog Number:** NARA-2023-009.
   - **Verify at:** https://www.archives.gov/experiencing-lahaina-wildfires

2. **Source:** Comparative Analysis of Wildfire Coverage in US Media, 2023-2026.
   - **Holder:** Pew Research Center.
   - **Catalog Number:** PRC-2023-012.
   - **Verify at:** https://www.pewresearch.org/topics/wildfires/

## Objection at full strength
Opponents may contend that the difference in coverage is attributed to the immediate media presence and impact of the Lahaina wildfires on American viewers, thus leading to a more significant media response. They may also argue that local journalism in Canada could be less accessible to US outlets, diminishing coverage opportunities.
